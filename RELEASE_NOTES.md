# My Wine Cellar v3.2.2 Preview 1 — Label Capture

## New
- Label capture is now the fallback when a barcode has no match, online lookup is unavailable, or a bottle has no barcode.
- New **Photograph label instead** action beside barcode controls.
- Front-label photos use the iPhone rear camera, are resized/compressed locally, and preview before saving.
- Clearer distinction between **no online match** and **online lookup temporarily unavailable**.

## Still deliberately manual
This preview captures and stores a clean label image. It does not yet identify the wine from the label; that is the goal of v3.2.3 Smart Recognition.
